        Phase 1 · Development workflow

        Five stages, one ward at a time.

        The map above is Stage 3 in progress. Here's the full sequence behind the Delhi Housing & Locality Index, from first pixel to public feedback loop.

        01
        Landing & animation layer
        A Next.js + Framer Motion hero sets the tone before any data loads — smooth on arrival, and built to hold up on both a phone on Rajouri Garden's metro platform and a desktop in a broker's office.
        02
        Geospatial setup — 290 wards
        Municipal ward boundaries are imported into PostgreSQL + PostGIS and rendered with Leaflet, click-or-hover across every MCD, NDMC and Cantonment ward, with a dropdown fallback for direct search by name or number.
        03
        Ward information aggregator
        One click opens a full dashboard: housing rate and trend, metro & bus connectivity, schools and hospitals with ratings, local markets, and a 3–5 image gallery of the locality — the panel on this page is its current shell.
        04
        Locality comparison engine
        Pick any two wards and benchmark them head-to-head — price per sq.ft, transit density, schooling options, healthcare ratings — side by side rather than tab by tab.
        05
        Interactive feedback module
        Residents and visitors submit corrections, ratings and local insight ward-by-ward, moderated before it goes public — so the index gets more accurate the more Delhi uses it.
        Next.js · Tailwind · Framer Motion FastAPI PostgreSQL + PostGIS AWS S3 / Cloudinary
